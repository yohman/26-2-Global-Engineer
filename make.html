Weekly experiments

Think with
your hands.

Small individual artifacts accumulate into a shared Atlas, sharper questions, and better group installations.

Week 1

ATLAS 01 — Engineering Nation

PromptChoose one country other than Japan. What did it engineer, for whom, and at what cost?

Minimum artifactMap/KML + 3–5 evidence points + one controversy.

Week 2

Local Spatial Problem

PromptIdentify one nearby issue a map could reveal or reframe.

Minimum artifactMap or sketch + data idea + stakeholder.

Week 3

Fix My Community

PromptUse data/GIS thinking to improve a public-service problem.

Minimum artifactMap, dashboard, app mockup, or annotated sketch.

Week 4

Redesign It

PromptRedesign one frustrating everyday interaction for an excluded user.

Minimum artifactBefore/after interface, flow, or physical prototype.

Week 5

My Doraemon

PromptDesign care technology for 2050. State its boundary.

Minimum artifactWHO / NEED / ROBOT / HUMAN card—and what it must never do.

Week 6

AI Impact Card

PromptChoose one AI use. Make its trade-offs inspectable.

Minimum artifactBenefit / harm / data / oversight / red line.

Week 7

Infrastructure Decision Map

PromptSpend ¥100B in Asia. Who gains, loses, and lives with it?

Minimum artifactWHERE / WHAT / WHY / BENEFICIARIES / 20-YEAR EFFECT.

Week 8

Appropriate Intervention

PromptRespond to a real place under severe constraints.

Minimum artifactPlace + constraint + durable intervention sketch.

Week 9

Field-Test Revision

PromptRevise a design after field realities interrupt it.

Minimum artifactFailure point + revised response.

Week 10

First 72 Hours

PromptMake something useful immediately after a disaster.

Minimum artifactMap, system, prototype, or information tool.

Week 11

Japan → World

PromptAdapt one Japanese idea for a global market.

Minimum artifactProduct/service concept + adaptation choices.

Weeks 12–14

Installation

PromptTurn research into a public argument with a product.

Minimum artifactPrototype → critique response → exhibition installation.
