## Overview / Big Question

A prototype invites a better conversation than a slide deck. What must change before the work meets a public?

## In Class

Five rotations: **+** something strong / **?** something unclear / **→** something to change.

## Scenario / Activity

Install your work at critique scale. Ask visitors to act, decide, or respond—not only listen.

## MAKE

Update your installation with documented critique responses. Final installation is due next week.
