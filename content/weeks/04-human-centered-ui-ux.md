## Overview / Big Question

Human-centered design and UI/UX. Who is excluded when a public interface is designed for an imaginary “average” user?

## Lecture

Bring your “Who is this designed for?” creation assigned in Week 3. Each student has about 15 minutes in total to share their inquiry and have a dialogue with the class. The form, scope, and balance of presenting and conversation are yours to choose.

## Participatory Class Activity

Redesign one public interface for five radically different users. Make the trade-offs visible.

## MAKE

**Redesign It.** Choose one frustrating everyday interaction and make a before/after interface, flow, or physical prototype.

## Homework

Revise from feedback. These short presentations are practice, not the mid-term. Explore the [individual mid-term and final installation brief](projects.html) and bring possible topics to Week 6.
