## Bio

Yoko Myers, GISP, is a GIS expert whose career spans nearly 20 years, evolving from GIS analyst to her current role as Senior IT Manager for the County of Santa Clara’s GIS team. In this position, she sets the countywide vision and strategy for enterprise GIS, leads governance efforts, and manages the daily operations of the GIS team.

Since 2016, Yoko has led major initiatives including formally approved IT standards for GIS, a three-year GIS roadmap, the Enterprise GIS Steering Committee, and high-impact projects such as parcel basemap modernization. She also oversees regional partnerships for imagery and LiDAR acquisition, advances safe use of AI in the ArcGIS platform, and provides advanced GIS solutions to 911 Communications, Emergency Management, and Public Health.

Passionate about public service, Yoko makes a meaningful difference in the community through spatial technologies. Her leadership emphasizes collaboration, strategic planning, and technical excellence across the County’s GIS ecosystem.

Yoko holds a Master of Public Policy from the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), and a Bachelor of Arts from Keio University in Tokyo.
