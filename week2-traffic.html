Sources and how to read the drawing

The 405 scene is a line-art illustration, not a live feed, vehicle count, or surveyed lane plan. Metro’s 2017 corridor study reported approximately 300,000 vehicles per day. The illustrated sixteen lanes are expressive, not an actual lane count. The comparison uses LA County and Tokyo Metropolis, not their entire metropolitan regions. It presents dated snapshots, not a causal ranking: demographics use the explicitly labelled census/estimate years; commute choices use 2011 LA workers and 2010 Tokyo work/school travellers; daily ridership uses 2019 agency records. Tokyo education uses imputed results and a different age base from the US ACS. Race and nationality are not interchangeable. Tokyo Metro excludes other Tokyo rail operators; LA bus/rail daily boardings are annual mode totals divided by 365. The equity research is from 2022. The tunnel is shown as a proposal.
