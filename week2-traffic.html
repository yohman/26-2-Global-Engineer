THE JOURNEY IS A SYSTEM

The tunnel is one segment.

A trip also includes getting to the station, waiting, transferring, and reaching the destination. A faster underground leg cannot answer those questions by itself.

A 2018 public comment from Culver City Councilmember Meghan Sahli-Wells asked whether the Westside proposal offered equitable access. That is a question to investigate—not a verdict on the technology.
