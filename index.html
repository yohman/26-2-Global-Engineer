Course promise

Engineering is not only technology.

It is a way to understand a place, meet real people, make choices under constraints, and build a response worth sharing.

Students will map systems, question working engineers, test ideas under real constraints, build weekly MAKE artifacts, and transform one group inquiry into a public installation. The goal: become more observant, critical, collaborative, and confident about acting as an engineer in the world.

This is a living course. Topics, guests, scenarios, and assignments may evolve as the semester takes shape.
