Course promise

Engineering is not only technology.

It is a way to understand a place, meet real people, make choices under constraints, and build a response worth sharing.

Each student leads a mid-term session and creates an individual final installation. Research across places, learn from working engineers, and make your own global engineering position tangible.

This is a living course. Topics, guests, scenarios, and assignments may evolve as the semester takes shape.
