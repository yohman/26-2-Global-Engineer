# Documentary assets for the UN story

These are original published photographs/artwork or a rendered published map, not generated reconstructions.

- `bentiu-dykes-2021.webp`: Captain Muhammad Junaid Ud Din / UNMISS, article dated 12 October 2021. https://unmiss.unmissions.org/en/news/pakistani-peacekeepers-serving-unmiss-construct-earthen-dykes-prevent-flooding-bentiu
- `bentiu-access-2021.webp`: Jacob Ruai / UNMISS, article dated 30 November 2021. https://unmiss.unmissions.org/en/news/unmiss-engineers-rapidly-respond-after-fresh-flooding-destroys-homes-and-cuts-access
- `bentiu-unosat-2021.webp`: UNITAR–UNOSAT product 3161. Sentinel-2 image 17 October 2021; publication 22 October 2021. Rendered full map, compressed to WebP. Zoom is a crop, not a new analysis. Red is detected water, not depth or confirmed road access. https://unosat.org/products/3161
- `south-sudan-flag-2011.jpg`: UN photograph published by UN News, South Sudan admission, 14 July 2011. https://news.un.org/en/story/2011/07/381552

- `srebrenica-memorial.jpg`: Memorial photograph published by EEAS in 2021. It depicts remembrance, not the 1995 killings. https://www.eeas.europa.eu/eeas/remembering-srebrenica-and-working-reconciliation-understand-heal-and-move-forward_en

## Membership data

Gold dots follow the UN Library's founding-members table, not position in the grid. The historical view has 51 named states. Today's 193-member roster includes 49 original memberships; Czechoslovakia and Yugoslavia are not assigned to modern successor states. Historical names/representation changes for China, USSR/Russia, Belarus and Ukraine are explained on selection.

## Interpretation

Discussion prompts are questions for the class. Reported choices are identified as reported, particularly the October 2021 transfer of engineering equipment. No invented staff testimony, operational route, voting record, flood footprint, or divorce rate is presented as evidence. Bentiu images dated 2021 are labelled as such even where the narrative discusses continued work reported in 2023.
