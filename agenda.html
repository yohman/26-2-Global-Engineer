Fourteen-week
agenda

This course investigates how engineering shapes communities, nations, and global systems. Through maps, guest conversations, field scenarios, weekly MAKE assignments, and a group installation, students will learn to frame real problems, work across cultures, judge trade-offs, and turn research into a tangible public argument.
