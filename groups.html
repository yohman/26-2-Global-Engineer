GROUP 01

Human-Centered Design / UI/UX

October 20 · Week 4

Teach
Compare a Japanese and international interface. Name the users left out.
Run
Public-interface redesign under accessibility and user-role cards.
Install
An interaction or prototype that makes exclusion visible—and proposes a better experience.
Bring first
One-page research frame + activity plan + rough prototype.
GROUP 02

AI, Data & Society

November 3 · Week 6

Teach
Frame one societal AI decision with evidence, risks, and accountability.
Run
Design or contest an AI grading system. Define appeal and human oversight.
Install
An interactive ethics decision, audit, or data-visibility artifact—not an AI demo alone.
Bring first
Position statement + system sketch + one hard question.
GROUP 03

International Development & Sustainability

November 17 · Week 8

Teach
Research a real community. Define what “appropriate” means there.
Run
Build under cost, power, language, maintenance, and exit constraints.
Install
A locally maintainable intervention model that shows trade-offs, not saviorism.
Bring first
Place profile + constraint set + response sketch.
GROUP 04

Disaster & Humanitarian Response

December 1 · Week 10

Teach
Trace a crisis through people, data, logistics, and ethics.
Run
First 72 Hours simulation. Change the situation with an aftershock.
Install
A response map, system, game, tool, or coordination prototype that adapts to disruption.
Bring first
Scenario pack + roles + response logic.
GROUP 05

Global Business & Innovation

December 15 · Week 12

Teach
Explain why a product changes across markets and cultures.
Run
Japan → World adaptation sprint with an assigned destination market.
Install
A market-aware product or service: value, UX, language, price, infrastructure, culture.
Bring first
Market research + adaptation decision + early product form.

PowerPoint is not the product.

Every group must make a tangible or interactive public-facing work. It can be an app, website, map, data visualization, model, interface, video, game, simulation, physical object, or detailed system design.

Final installation check: Question → Research → Position → Response → Product.
